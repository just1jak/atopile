# Third-party hardware-library material

Seeed Studio XIAO ESP32-S3 symbol/footprint are derived from Seeed-Studio/OSHW-XIAO-Series (https://github.com/Seeed-Studio/OSHW-XIAO-Series), Copyright(c)2024Seeed-Projects, MIT. The full notice is in source/Seeed-LICENSE-MIT.txt. Unmodified source definitions are included. The14-pin subset, through-hole socket-only pads,1.0mm drills/1.8mm lands, and USB overhang silk trimming are the local modifications.

Other schematic symbols, PCB footprints, and render models originate from the installed official KiCad10 libraries, contributors to the KiCad libraries. KiCad libraries use CC-BY-SA4.0 with an exception allowing their use in electronic designs without forcing the entire design to that license. Retain source/KiCad-LICENSE.md and the original library attribution. Embedded symbol definitions and footprints are included in native KiCad files. The preview uses installed KiCad3D models; the removable XIAO/socket assembly is not rendered.

No authentication data, firmware signing keys, or private scanner project files are embedded in this carrier package.
