# Chrysler scanner - revision C procurement prototype

Open `ChryslerScanner_V240_SD.kicad_pro` in KiCad 10 with its whole folder. The 24-page schematic PDF starts with the system block diagram and 168 native hierarchical ports. Input connector and protection share one sheet. The design retains CCD, PCI and SCI/programming interfaces. CAN hardware is deferred by user decision; `reference/can-upgrade.md` describes the next revision.

The CCD transmit buffers now use exact TTL-input AHCT devices at 5 V: Nexperia 74AHCT1G17GVH and TI SN74AHCT1G14DBVR. Their gate resistors are 330 ohms. Q1/Q6/Q16 and Q7 have the correct SOT-363 lands; Q2/Q3/Q4 use low-threshold DMN62D2UDM-7 devices on their original SOT-23-6 lands. The revised diode selections match their physical packages. C14 has a 50 V 1210 capacitor and manufacturer-specific lands. Main power inductors are Eaton DR74-220-R; filter inductors are exact 240/470 nH Bourns SRN2012 parts, preserving the original filter values.

`BOM.csv` records all 329 electrical/probe entries with exact manufacturer identities, minimum/selected voltage ratings, tolerance, source and assembly classification. `assembly-BOM.csv` groups the 321 parts to purchase/place. R23/R24 are explicitly DNP. JP1/JP2 and TP45-TP48 are PCB features, not purchased components. Placement exports are checked against exactly those 321 placed parts. Review-selected identities are not a claim of guaranteed stock or automotive qualification; verify supplier ordering codes and lifecycle when purchasing. ESP32-PICO-V3-02 is an existing NRND design choice.

The board remains 110 x 55 mm, four layers, nominal 1.6 mm. The nine zone definitions, outline, holes, rules and all retained copper remain unchanged. The procurement delta adds 46 tracks and removes nine specifically recorded unlocked branch/stub objects. It adds no drills and changes no via-processing flags. C14, L3 and L5 have documented local placement/routing changes. Four small MOSFET land patterns and the inductor/capacitor maximum-body envelopes are corrected. Frozen historical safety and testpoint audits remain intact and are rerun separately from the exact procurement-delta audit.

`reference/p1-contract-audit.json` is the combined release check for the actual PCB hash. It must report PASS and `releaseGatePassed: true`. Fresh all-severity ERC and full DRC/parity must have zero findings/unrouted items. The independent procurement review is hash-bound to the actual schematic and PCB; its scope is a delta review layered over earlier reviews. Editing CAD invalidates the reports and exports. Original Atopile sources do not regenerate this native revision.

Use only this package's Gerbers/job, separate PTH/NPTH drills, BOM, positions and processing notes. All 230 recorded filled, copper-capped and planarized sites are mandatory; open or tented vias are not equivalent. Agree actual RF/USB stackup and impedance with the fabricator before ordering. No manufacturing order or external fabrication upload is performed by the exporter.

This is a bounded prototype, not a tested or automotive-qualified product. Normal input is 9-16 V; protected-input/VPP peaks must remain <=28 V including overshoot within `reference/rev-c-safety-contract.md`. Sustained 24 V/jump start is unsupported. Hardware checks remain for protection energy, thermal/current limits, powered/unpowered faults, startup/decay, watchdog timing, gate waveforms, analog calibration, bus ESD and regulator stability. C14's reference bias curve is about 8 uF at nominal 20.6 V and 5.5 uF at 28 V; it does not guarantee a minimum effective capacitance. Measure actual output ripple, transient response and stability. Eaton's retained pads cover its actual feet but differ from the larger suggested lands; verify assembly/fillets and temperature on the prototype.

GPIO13/pad20 ACT_LED belongs exclusively to the supervised software health task: pulses every 10 ms only while required tasks vote healthy. No SD/activity writers, autonomous blink/PWM/DMA or legacy firmware may drive it. GPIO2 remains NC. Firmware integration and ADC calibration remain deferred and are required before active operation. Hardware cannot distinguish an unrelated software pulse from a valid heartbeat.

Mechanical sample fit remains required. M3 socket head, no washer: checked 5.5 mm head x 3 mm height, with H1 clearance 0.7504 mm centered / 0.2504 mm after nominal screw float. C61 is TOP at (193,127) mm, nominal 5.9 mm high; allow component tolerances. Main inductors need 4.35 mm maximum height, C14 2.8 mm; C1 is the taller radial capacitor and needs its selected manufacturer's height tolerance. Provide underside microSD/card access and at least the previously specified 7 mm underside enclosure space, then check actual samples, hook access and enclosure tolerances.
